Market List – Privacy Policy

Market List – Shopping by DK · com.dk.marketlist

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Market List ("the app") is a shopping-list app developed by DK. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your phone

The shopping lists and items you type are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your phone unless you choose to send them.

Sending lists by WhatsApp

When you tap Send on WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp (or another app you pick) with the list text ready to send. You choose the contact and decide whether to send. After that, the message is handled by WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. The app itself has no internet permission and cannot send anything on its own.

If you save a WhatsApp number in Settings, it is stored only on your device and is used only to open that chat.

Receiving lists

When you share a WhatsApp message into the app or paste copied text, the app reads only that text to create the list. It does not read your other messages, contacts or files.

Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste copied message.

Voice typing

If you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech-recognition service (for example Google) converts your voice to text. That service's privacy policy applies. The app receives only the resulting text.

Backup

Backup saves a file to a location you choose. Restore reads a file you choose. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete any list inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
